# Bleach / Ichigo visual research

## Canon framing

VIZ describes Ichigo as a part-time student and full-time Soul Reaper who becomes a guardian of the afterlife. The origin is rooted in seeing ghosts, an encounter with Rukia Kuchiki, absorbing her Soul Reaper powers while fighting a Hollow, and taking responsibility for protecting people and helping spirits find peace.

The official Bleach anime site organizes the world into distinct realms: World of the Living, Soul Society, Royal Palace, Wandenreich, and Hueco Mundo. This supports a portfolio information architecture based on realms / transmissions / thresholds rather than generic portfolio sections.

## Visual translation for this portfolio

Use an original, Ichigo-inspired palette: deep ink black and charcoal as the base; vivid orange as the signature energy accent; bone white for blades, type, and mask surfaces; restrained crimson for danger and Hollow pressure; and cool steel blue for spirit-world depth. The current red/gold-only theme reads more like generic dark fantasy, so orange must become the dominant identity accent.

Use Soul Reaper motifs as abstract interface grammar rather than copied artwork: oversized diagonal blade cuts, reiatsu pressure lines, black shihakusho-like fields, white mask fragments, vertical Japanese-inspired indexing, spirit-world realm labels, and a two-state human / reaper threshold. The mask and Bankai toggles should be framed as original transformation states of the archive, not character replicas.

Ichigo’s narrative arc suggests a stronger site flow: ordinary signal -> awakening -> realm crossing -> inner conflict / Hollow pressure -> release / Bankai -> open transmission. The portfolio should use this arc to frame Sugumaran’s progression from BCA graduate to MCA / AI-ML and full-stack developer.

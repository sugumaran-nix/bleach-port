# Bleach-inspired portfolio visual brief

## Purpose
Create original visual assets for a premium anime portfolio homepage. The site should feel like a cinematic spiritual-world interface rather than a fan-art page.

## Art direction
A dark editorial anime atmosphere with black ink, smoky charcoal, bone white, deep reiatsu crimson, and muted spirit gold. Use abstract spiritual pressure, paper grain, calligraphic brush marks, katana arcs, fragmented manga panels, floating dust, and a distant moonlit city/temple silhouette. The visual language should be original and avoid copyrighted characters, recognizable costumes, exact logos, or copied manga panels.

## Hero asset
Generate a wide 16:9 cinematic background with a large amount of negative space on the left for typography. On the right, place an original faceless shinigami-like silhouette in a long black robe with a sheathed sword, standing in a wind-swept night environment. Add a red crescent energy arc, thin gold linework, drifting ash particles, subtle paper texture, and atmospheric depth. No text, no logo, no UI, no recognizable existing character.

## Supporting assets
Create a small set of abstract, reusable textures: black ink wash, red reiatsu smoke, gold brush stroke, paper grain, and a monochrome spirit seal. They should work as subtle background layers or section separators, with transparent-looking edges where possible.

## Acceptance criteria
The assets must be elegant, cinematic, high contrast, text-safe, consistent as a set, and suitable for a responsive Next.js portfolio. They should enhance the interface without becoming a literal anime screenshot.
